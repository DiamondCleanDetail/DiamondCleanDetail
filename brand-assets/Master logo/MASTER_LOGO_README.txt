For embroidery, vinyl cutting, engraving, screen printing and supplier-ready EPS/SVG artwork, a separate clean flat vector construction of the exact approved geometry should be created and checked against this master before production.

MASTER RULE
Do not stretch, skew, redraw, recolor, alter proportions, change spacing, or modify the diamond/DC geometry without creating a new approved master.
